Our school

A school built on belief.

Since 2004, Pakistan Scholars High School has grown with the families of District Mandi Bahauddin — one classroom, one question and one small victory at a time.

Across two campuses, we make room for curiosity, encourage honest effort and help every student take their next step with confidence.
